# Pro Shop pavilion v1: first detailed model (Art concept A)

**Status: first detailed Blender model for Art and Chase review. It is not an accepted final.**

This is the generic Mini Golf V2 lobby pro shop, built in the selected Art concept A style (sage and
timber park pavilion). `inputs/A-park-pavilion.jpg` is the aesthetic authority. The Art parts guide and
handoff checklist were used for structure; their 20 x 12 x 12 dimensions are proposals, not measurements.
The screen shows an **illustrative UI placeholder only**. There is no commerce, prices, currency, course
selection, NPC or mirror. The real UX is the builder's later job.

Rebuild: `src\run_all.cmd [blender.exe] [concept.jpg]`. It runs generate, validate, render (CPU, 64
samples, denoised), FBX export and the contact sheet, one Blender process at a time. The individual
commands are in each script's docstring. The geometry is deterministic (`--seed 7`).

## Conventions
- 1 Blender unit = 1 stud. Z is up. The open walk-up front faces -Y. The FBX export uses forward -Z, up Y,
  scale 1.0 and embedded textures.
- The asset origin is the ground centre of the plinth footprint. Every export mesh has identity
  rotation and scale, and its origin is at its own bounding-box base centre.
- One material per export mesh. Every mesh has a UV map: board, beam and tile pieces use box
  projection along the piece's long axis, with a per-piece offset so boards vary. Spheres, prints
  and the display use explicit UVs. Small props use palette cells.
- The largest mesh is about 4.5k tris (`Roof_Tiles_L/R`). The export total is about 35k tris in 104 meshes.

## Scale decisions (modeler's, honest)
- **Width**: the plinth is 20 wide. The eaves reach 22.6 wide including the barge boards.
- **Depth**: the plinth is 9.4 deep. The roof overhang is 10.5 deep. The total including the level
  approach apron is 13.6 deep. I kept the body shallower than the proposed 12 so the rear screen and
  sign stay readable from the counter, about 9 to 10 studs from a 5-stud eye. That matches the
  concept's close view.
- **Ridge**: the ridge cap is about 12.7 and the finial top is 13.05. I raised it slightly above the
  proposed 12 to keep the concept's steeper front gable (28° pitch). Twelve on a 20-wide body would
  flatten the silhouette.
- **Counter**: the top is 3.15, which is 3.03 above the approach. The approach is a flagstone ramp
  rising 0.12 over 3.35 studs (2°) that meets the plinth flush, so there is no step.
- **Openings**: the front opening is clear to 6.25 under the tie beam. The plinth top is 0.12.

## Structure (collections)
- `PS_Structure`: stone plinth, approach base and flagstones, 4 chunky bevelled posts with shoes, front
  and rear tie beams, side plates, front angled braces, front and rear king posts, ridge beam, rafters
  (including gable top chords), mid collar truss, base, waist and eave-frieze trims, interior floor planks.
- `PS_Walls`: side and rear walls in individual vertical boards, sage below the waist rail and cream
  above. The front gable has cream boards. Board widths vary slightly, with chamfered seams.
- `PS_Windows`: a window in each side wall with frame, mullions, inner and outer casings, sill and
  glass. The left window has a bracketed flower box.
- `PS_Roof`: per side, deck and soffit planks, underlay, and 12 staggered overlapping tile courses
  with a starter strip. Each tile is tilted onto the course below. There are layered fascia boards.
  The ridge cap is made of overlapping V pieces. Front and rear outer and inner barge boards and finials.
- `PS_Counter`: sage board front, kick and apron trims, a thick bevelled lip, and top planks.
- `PS_BallTray` (LEFT): rim, sage felt and shallow dividers in 8 cells. `PS_Items_Balls` holds 8 ball
  skins (classic white, royal blue, violet swirl, lime, coral split, sunny dots, teal band, pink
  quarters) plus the white active-preview ball in the dish.
- `PS_Dish` (CENTRE): a square inset that flows into a rounded raised rim, then a shallow charcoal
  bowl 0.35 deep.
- `PS_ClubRack` (RIGHT): cheek, base, base lip, sage back panel and slotted comb rail.
  `PS_Items_Clubs` holds 6 putters: blade, mallet and heel-toe heads, a ferrule, and coloured
  grips (purple, blue, yellow, black, pink, teal). The grips rest on the rail and the heads sit on
  the base.
- `PS_Decor`: rear shelf and brackets, screen bezel and backing, the framed PRO SHOP sign (mesh
  lettering), two framed golf prints with mats, and two shelf plants.
- `PS_UI_Display`: `UI_Display_Surface_REPLACEABLE`, a separate quad with UV 0..1 and its front facing -Y.
  Attach the real SurfaceGui here.
- Not exported:
  - `PS_UI_Placeholder_ILLUSTRATIVE`: Balls/Clubs tabs, Buy / Owned-Equip / Equipped cards, a separate
    Equipped Ball and Club readout, and an on-screen "UI PLACEHOLDER" note.
  - `PS_Anchors`: 22 empties.
  - `PS_Collision_Proxy`: 14 `COL_*` boxes and prisms, wire display, hidden in renders.
  - `PS_Source_EditableText`: the editable PRO SHOP text curve.
  - `ENV_RenderOnly`: ground, trees, sun, interior fill light and cameras.

To change the sign text, edit `Sign_Lettering_TEXTSOURCE` (or the generator), then convert it to a mesh
and replace `Sign_Lettering`.

## Anchors
- UI: `ANCHOR_UI_ScreenPanel` (stores width/height, faces -Y), `ANCHOR_UI_EquippedBall`,
  `ANCHOR_UI_EquippedClub`.
- Display: `ANCHOR_Display_ActivePreview` (bottom of the dish), `ANCHOR_BallTray_Slot_01..08`,
  `ANCHOR_ClubRack_Slot_01..06`.
- Interaction and reference: `ANCHOR_Prompt_Counter`, `ANCHOR_Player_StandSpot`, `ANCHOR_Sign`,
  `ANCHOR_Root_BaseCentre`.

Positions are in `manifest.json`.

## Materials (SurfaceAppearance-ready)
- Building:
  - `M_Wood_Warm`, `M_Wood_Dark`, `M_Paint_Cream`, `M_Paint_Sage`, `M_Roof_Tile_Sage` and
    `M_Stone_Flag` each use one 512 px colour map.
- Props:
  - `M_Props_Palette` uses Color, Metalness and Roughness maps (256 px, 8x8 palette cells).
  - `M_Ball_Skins` uses a 1024x512 atlas.
  - `M_Art_Prints` uses a 512x256 map.
- Display:
  - `M_UI_Display_Placeholder` is flat dark.

Colour, metalness and roughness come only from the maps. There is no modeled grain or dimple
geometry; the dimples are a faint printed pattern on the ball texture only.

## Known limits and notes for review
- Side and rear placement (windows, rear wall, depth) is inferred, as the guide states. The rear
  exterior is deliberately plain boards with a timber frame.
- The eye-level counter view (28 mm) cannot show the tray, dish and rack together at a natural
  distance. The concept's close view is ultra-wide. The tray and rack therefore have their own
  eye-level views.
- The putter heads are original, stylised shapes, not brand replicas.
- The UI placeholder is not a UX spec.
