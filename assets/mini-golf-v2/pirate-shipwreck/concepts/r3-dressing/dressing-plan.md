# Pirate Shipwreck: density and dressing pass (Game Art Bot, Oct 31 2026)
Rule of thumb for every hole: 3 layers in view.
- Foreground (0-15 studs past the 6-stud clear zone): small clutter clusters at the pad corners.
- Midground (15-80 studs): one set piece per hole.
- Backdrop (80+ studs): a silhouette on the skyline.
No bare ground larger than about 20x20 studs within sight of a pad. Pads stay bright, and nothing goes inside 6 studs of felt.

## Kit (priority order)
P1 (biggest win, built as modular sets and reused everywhere)
1. Palm clusters: 3 heights (25/40/60) plus bent or leaning versions, and a ground-fern skirt mesh. 40-60 placed.
2. Rock clusters: 3 sizes of warm stone (115,100,92) with sandy tops, half-buried. 30-50 placed.
3. Jungle undergrowth: big-leaf plants, ferns, banana leaves, hanging vines. 60+ placed in the jungle.
4. Wreck debris kit: broken planks, barrels, crates, a rope coil, a snapped spar and a half-buried hull rib. 25-40 placed, mostly on the beach and wreck.
5. Backdrop: 2-3 distant sea stacks and islands (low poly, hazy) plus an offshore reef ring, so the horizon isn't empty.
P2
6. Beach: driftwood, shells and starfish decals, tide-line wet-sand decal strips, a tidepool rock ring and seaweed.
7. Path dressing: rope-and-post fence, plank boardwalk sections in the jungle, and torches or lanterns (warm PointLight R12).
8. Story props: a treasure-map sign by each tee, a "Dig Here" X, a bottle with a message, a skull-free jolly-roger rag on a stick, and a rowboat.
9. Cave approach: stalagmite clusters, rope bridge railings, a mine-cart track stub, glowing crystals or mushrooms (Neon, small), and a gold-coin spill.
P3
10. Ambient life: gulls (bobbing), a crab pair, butterflies, fireflies near the cave, and a waterfall mist ParticleEmitter.
11. Terrain paint: break up the grass with mud, ground and sand blends, and add flower and leaf decals.

## Per zone
- Beach and wreck (pads 1-9): debris trail from the wreck up the sand, 2 palm clusters per hole, rock clusters at the waterline, a rowboat and barrels at pad 1, a reef and sea stacks behind, and lanterns on wreck posts.
- Jungle trail (10-14): a dense canopy on both sides (aim for about 70% of the side view filled), a vine arch over the path every ~40 studs, boardwalk bits over mud, the flytrap and croc as set pieces, and god-ray shafts through gaps.
- Cave approach (15-17): the jungle thins into rock, with stalagmites, a fallen-palm bridge, torch pairs leading in, and the waterfall mist.
- Cave (18): crystals, a coin spill, the captain set piece and the warm glow, plus 2 bats on the ceiling.
## Lighting accents
Lanterns and torches for the pad PointLights, crystal glows in the cave, SunRays in the jungle, and fireflies near the cave.

## Elevation (added after the Walkabout review)
- Raise each pad on a low plinth or ledge (2-6 studs) with a drop-off on at least one side. Use terrain rock or sand berms, not flat fields.
- Beach: dunes and rock shelves stepping down to the water. Jungle: the trail cut into a slope, with banks 8-15 studs high on one side. Cave approach: terraces stepping up to the mouth.
- Themed rims (logs, planks, rope posts, carved stone) are hole-time work, not part of this pass.
