# Pirate Shipwreck: round 1 style notes (world only)
Story: a pirate galleon ran aground; players follow the crew's trail wreck -> beach -> jungle -> cave. Cartoony skeletons, no gore.
Route: one walking trail, zones open up around corners. 18 pads: 1-4 wreck deck & cove, 5-9 beach, 10-14 jungle, 15-18 cave cliff.
Rules: every pad on its own built plank deck or stone slab; scenery around pads, never on them; big pieces keep a clear ring (~36 map px, roughly 2.2+ studs clearance at cups, confirm in studs at layout step). Art never stands in for a gameplay surface.
Landmarks visible from most holes: wreck mast (start), cave cliff with waterfall (finish).
Shape language: chunky low-poly, rounded planks, oversized props. Palette per look: see look A/B/C files. Original design (rubber-duck figurehead, patchwork sails); no Walkabout/Pirates/One Piece shapes.
Hero meshes for 3D Model Bot (after Chase picks): wreck, cave cliff, gags 1, 2, 7, 10, 11, 13. Gags 3-6, 8, 9, 12 are dressing.
Tools: make_map.py, make_sheets.py regenerate the overlays from raw/.
