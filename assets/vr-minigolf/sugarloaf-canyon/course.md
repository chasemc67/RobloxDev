# Sugarloaf Canyon: Theme Bible

VR mini golf course for Roblox. **18 holes, total par 54** (front 27, back 27; H4 is par 3 in p7, H15 remains par 3; see `playtest-log.md`). It's a candy-striped desert canyon at sunset: stacked
sandstone mesas banded pink and cream, rock-candy crystals growing out of the cliffs, saguaros wearing swirl lollipops and gumball fruit,
rope bridges, a peppermint dam with a 91-stud spillway, and Old Sugarloaf, a lone striped dome on the skyline.
Reference art: `../store/ui/card_soon_sugarloafcanyon_color_clean.png` (menu card).

Status: **all 18 holes specced and sim-tuned.** Hole geometry is in `holes.json` (generated by `tools/build_holes.py`), with per-hole
`hNN-notes.md` and `hNN-layout.png`, `course-map.png`, `holes_layouts_sheet.jpg`, and every tuning round in `playtest-log.md`. The world
layout is in `world/world.json` (placements, footprints, walkways), checked in `world/validation.md` (CLEAN, including "every holes.json
felt and rail corner lies inside its footprint"). Next: Studio build and calibration of the five moving obstacles and the lift.

Units: studs. **1 stud = 0.3 m**, Y up, +X east, -Z north. Physics is the same as Lantern Grove (see `tools/README.md`): g 32.7,
rolling deceleration 0.5 + 0.25·v, rails 0.63, bumpers 0.65, cup capture < 5.33 studs/s, cup radius 0.5, ball radius 0.08,
max putt 19.78 studs/s (60 studs on flat felt). Lanes 7-8 wide, greens 12-18, rails 1 high and 0.5 thick.

## Mood

It's warm, golden and a bit silly, like a Wild West postcard drawn by a candy maker. The rock is real-looking sandstone, but its strata run in
peppermint and taffy colours. Crystals are rock candy, cacti carry lollipops, the hoodoos wear gumdrop caps and the dunes are powdered sugar.
Nothing is dangerous: water is clear turquoise, drops have rope railings, and the old mine and ranger station feel like a friendly theme park.
**Scale sells it in VR.** Slot walls rise 44-52 studs (13-16 m) on either side of you, mesa cliffs are 34-50 studs tall, and Old Sugarloaf
stands 110 studs (33 m) above the canyon floor. The player starts at the bottom of the canyon and ends on top of the highest mesa
watching golden light over a far-off natural arch. The separate hero-render sun is aligned horizontally with the arch; its requested +2° elevation is 1° above the opening.

## World arc (6 zones, low to high)

| Zone | Holes | Ground y | Where | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sugarloaf Junction** | H1-H3 | 18 (canyon floor) | South-centre, both banks of the Sugarloaf River | Frontier boardwalk town, mine-head, ranger station. Teaches banks, route choice and water |
| **Rock Candy Narrows** | H4-H6 | 18 / 22 / 26 | A slot canyon cut into Taffy Mesa | Enclosed, cool and echoing. Glittering crystal walls, a ribbon of pink sky. First timing obstacle |
| **Taffy Mesa** | H7-H9 | 70 | First mesa top, east | Big open sky again. Rope bridges over slots, gumdrop boulders, a windpump |
| **Sugarloaf Dam** | H10-H12 | 116 (Reservoir Bench) | North: the reservoir and Peppermint Dam | Engineering whimsy: sluices, locks, an intake tower. Walk across the dam crest past the spillway |
| **Hoodoo Garden** | H13-H15 | 150 (Hoodoo Bench) | North-west third tier | Striped hoodoo spires, the old ore cableway, powdered-sugar dunes |
| **Sugarloaf Crown** | H16-H18 | 190 (top tier) | South-west, the highest mesa | Summit finale: sinkhole, sundial, Sunset Arch. Sunset Gondola back to town |

The route goes up in steps: floor (18) → slot (26) → mesa (70) → bench (116) → hoodoo bench (150) → crown (190). Each step up is one
lift ride with a teleport alternative. The views open up with height: from the Crown you can see every earlier zone. The Sunset Gondola
then carries the player 172 studs down to the plaza where they started, so the course is a loop.

## Materials (Roblox)

- **Felt**: SmoothPlastic or Fabric with a subtle noise texture, no reflectance. Inside the Narrows use the sage felt so the slot reads cooler.
- **Rails**: visual meshes are chunky pink sandstone blocks with timber caps. Collision is invisible parts from holes.json, exactly as in Lantern Grove.
- **Terrain**: Sandstone for cliffs and mesa tops, Sand on the canyon floor and dunes, Ground for walkways. The candy stripes are a cliff
  **dressing kit** of striped rock-face meshes placed over the terrain cliffs, banded by world y every 6 studs using strata colours 1-6 in order.
  Painting by height gives you continuous stripes across every cliff.
- **Rock candy**: Glass, Transparency 0.25, Reflectance 0.1, with a faint Neon inner core so the crystals glow at sunset.
- **Water**: Terrain water, colour `#3FB7C9`, WaterTransparency 0.6. The spillway and falls are Beams with foam particles.
- **Peppermint dam and sluices**: SmoothPlastic red and white stripes, concrete texture on the faces.
- **Lollipops and gumballs**: SmoothPlastic, slightly glossy (Reflectance 0.05).

## Lighting (sunset, launch)

- In-game golden-hour lighting uses **bearing 277°, elevation 25°**, from `world.json sun`. ClockTime ~16.3 is a starting estimate; calibrate latitude and time in Studio to the actual direction.
- `world.json hero_render_sun` is **render-only: bearing 277°, elevation +2°**. The arch opening spans −12°..+1°, so this requested sun is **1° above its top**, not inside; never substitute it for the game sun.
- Ambient `#7A4A50`, OutdoorAmbient `#C9806A`, Brightness 2.0, ColorShift_Top `#FFC38A`, ColorShift_Bottom `#B45A7A`.
- Atmosphere: Density 0.35, Offset 0.1, Color `#F7B48E`, Decay `#B4699A`, Glare 0.6, Haze 2.0 (lilac distance, like the card).
- Bloom 0.7 / threshold 1.3, SunRays 0.12. Soft long shadows; the slot canyon floors are in shadow, lit by bounce light only (warm pink).
- Crystals and lantern-cacti PointLights are subtle (brightness 0.5). A later **night variant** (blue-violet sky, glowing crystals, star field) can reuse them.

## Landmarks

1. **Old Sugarloaf**: a free-standing striped dome in the canyon, 144 studs across, summit y 128. It is the skyline anchor; the Addendum 5 height makes it hidden from H17/H18 tees as well as the three enclosed slot-canyon tees (recorded by world validation).
2. **Peppermint Dam and Spillway Falls**: a red-and-white arched dam holding Sugarloaf Reservoir (water y 108). A 20-wide spillway sheet
   drops 91 studs into the gorge. You walk across its crest between H11 and H12.
3. **Sugarloaf Crown**: the top mesa tier (y 190) with **Sunset Arch** on its west rim and a giant **sundial**.
4. **Rock Candy Narrows**: a slot canyon with crystal-studded walls, 44 wide and 44-52 deep.
5. **Junction town**: false-front shops, a saloon-style clubhouse, a candy-striped water tower and the mine-head.
6. **Licorice Falls** (45 → 16) and **Fizzwater Falls** (148 → 114): ribbons of water off the mesa edges.
7. **Far horizon**: lilac distant mesas, a huge natural arch on the west horizon (the hero sun is at its bearing, with the +2° elevation mismatch flagged above), and slow
   candy-striped hot-air balloons.

## The 18 holes (concepts)

Each hole has one headline gimmick and none repeats. Footprints (W x L studs) are reserved in world.json; pars are design targets that
the sim will confirm later.

**Par spread:** three par 2s (H1, H10, H16), twelve par 3s, three par 4s (H7, H12, H18). Total **54**:
Junction 8, Narrows 9, Taffy Mesa 10, Dam 9, Hoodoo 9, Crown 9. H4 increases from par 2 to 3 in p7, restoring 54. H15 retains its sim-tested par 3 (see `playtest-log.md` Round 4).

**Gimmick families**, so the variety is visible:
- static geometry: bank (H1), crystal bumpers (H4), half-pipe (H5), rolling dips (H8), pillar maze (H13), side slope (H15);
- level changes: one-way chute (H10), lock elevator (H11), sinkhole drop (H16);
- route choice: tunnels (H2), river plank vs bridge (H3), pipe teleport vs shoreline (H12);
- timing obstacles, each with a different motion: slide with dwell (H6), vertical rotor (H9), pendulum (H14), horizontal sweeper (H17);
- spans: rope bridges (H7);
- finale: multi-gimmick (H18).

## Difficulty curve

- **H1-H3 (learn)**: one idea each: bank, route choice, water with a forgiving respawn. Par 2-3, wide felt (8), flags visible from every tee.
- **H4-H6 (read)**: angles and walls in the enclosed slot. The first timing obstacle is H6, which is slow with long, readable pauses.
- **H7-H9 (commit)**: the first par 4 with bridge spans, then pace control (dips) and a second timing obstacle (windpump).
- **H10-H12 (systems)**: one-way chute, lock elevator and pipe teleport, which teach that the course itself moves the ball.
- **H13-H15 (precision)**: maze lines, pendulum timing and side-slope reading: three demanding par 3s.
- **H16-H18 (payoff)**: a short, dramatic par 2, a sweeper green and a showpiece finale. It should feel grand but not punishing (target
  good-player average at or under par + 0.7).

Same sim targets as Lantern Grove: good HIO 0.2-10% with a brute-force ace line on every hole; good average between par − 0.7 and par + 1;
skill gap (average − good) ≥ 0.3; average ≤ par + 1.5; stroke cap ≤ 1%. Timing obstacles block the intended line in 25-50% of phases
with 0 traps, aiming the sim ~10 points above the in-engine target until Studio calibrates it.

## VR comfort

- **No forced locomotion.** Every walkway is optional to walk; teleport points sit at every zone hub (`world.json teleport_points`: Junction Plaza,
  Narrows Mouth, Taffy Lift Top, Dam East Overlook, Hoodoo Lift Top, Crown Lift Top) and the hole-to-hole "next tee" teleport stays available.
- **Lifts and gondola**: constant slow speed (lift 4 studs/s = 1.2 m/s, gondola 6 studs/s), no acceleration jolts, solid floor and railings,
  the horizon visible throughout, and a "teleport instead" button at both ends. The player never moves while turning.
- **Rails at drops**: the validator puts a railing on any walkway side where the ground falls more than 4 studs within 2.5 studs of its
  edge, and on every bridge and the dam crest. Flagged segments are `rails` in world.json.
- **Gentle walking**: ramps ≤ 10%, stairs ≤ 0.8 rise with 1.5 treads, ≤ 10 steps per flight, 4-stud landings.
- **Sun not in your eyes**: the game sun is at bearing 277 and 25° up. No tee plays within 45° of it: every hole plays north, south or east.
  The sunset is a side or back view while putting and the set-piece view from greens and overlooks (validated per hole).
- **Big drops are framed, not looked down into from the tee**: H10's chute and H16's sinkhole are 3.6 studs, with felt arrows. The 91-stud
  spillway is seen from the railed dam crest, not from a tee.
- **Slot canyon light**: keep the Narrows floor bright enough (bounce light, glowing crystals) so the ball is always readable.
- **Head clearance**: nothing hangs lower than 8 studs (2.4 m) over any felt or walkway except the moving obstacles themselves, which
  stay below 4 studs.
- **Flags**: 7.5-stud flag poles on every cup. H5 (half-pipe bend) and H13 (maze) may hide the flag by design; give them a lantern or crystal cue over the green.

## Build notes for the builder

- Place holes with `holeRoot = CFrame.new(origin) * CFrame.Angles(0, math.rad(yaw_deg), 0)` from world.json (= `world_offset` / `world_yaw` in
  holes.json). Build collision straight from holes.json (invisible, Anchored, CanCollide; felt `center` = top surface).
- Terrain: see `world/README.md` (heightmap mapping, region order, water levels, lifts).
- Moving parts: H6 sluice gate, H9 windpump, H11 lock gate + lift platform, H14 ore bucket, H17 sundial arms. Collision parts must be **CanCollide and moved
  every frame** (CFrame per Heartbeat or a constraint), following holes.json motion profiles including `dwell` pauses.
