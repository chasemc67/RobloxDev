**Part 2: Sugarloaf Canyon** (in `sugarloaf-canyon/`)
- **`course.md`**: theme bible built from the menu card (candy-striped mesas, rock-candy crystals, lollipop cacti, Peppermint Dam), with palette, landmarks, set pieces, a 6-zone arc, difficulty curve and VR comfort rules.
- **`world/world.json`**, generated by `world/tools/build_world.py`:
  - **Size and tiers:** 1280 × 1280 studs. Ground levels are canyon floor 18, slot floors 18–26, Taffy Mesa 70, Reservoir Bench 116 (water 108), Hoodoo Bench 150 and Sugarloaf Crown 190.
  - **Old Sugarloaf:** a free-standing dome whose summit is about y 208, the landmark seen from the tees.
  - **Water and crossings:**
    - three slot canyons and the Sugarloaf River;
    - two creeks, the reservoir behind Peppermint Dam, and four waterfalls (including a 91-stud spillway);
    - two bridges (one rope bridge) and a walk across the dam crest.
  - **Getting between holes:** 4 lifts, a gondola from the top back to the start plaza, 6 teleport points, and railings wherever a walkway edge drops more than 4 studs.
  - **Holes:** each one is a `concept_footprint` with a placement and flat pad boxes. Two holes declare spans: H3 over the river and H7 over Taffy Gap.
- **`world/validation.md`**: the validator checks footprint overlap, footprints on flat ground, path slopes, stairs, lifts and railings, path endpoints at cup and tee, that no tee faces the setting sun, and that Old Sugarloaf is visible from the tees.
  - The 3 warnings are the slot-canyon tees (H4–H6), which can't see Old Sugarloaf by design.
  - The first run caught a real bug: river banks with no distance limit were shaving down the dome and the bench. They now stop at a set reach.
  - Walking from spawn to the last cup is 2,687 studs (about 806 m).
- **`world/world-map.png`**, `world-map-base.png` (no text, for concept art), `heightmap.png` (16-bit, 1 px = 1 stud, y = gray/65535 × 260) and `heightmap-8bit.png`, all from `world/tools/make_maps.py`.
- **`world/README.md`**: conventions, placement table, terrain job list for the builder, and a scenery list for the 3D modeler.
- **`tools/`**: the Lantern Grove sim and its physics tests (all 13 pass on the copy), plus the timing, teleport and layout tools. These wait for a `holes.json`, which doesn't exist yet. The timing-obstacle list in `timing_check.py` is empty until the holes are built.
