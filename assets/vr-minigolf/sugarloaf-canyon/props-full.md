# Sugarloaf Canyon: Consolidated Prop List (for 3D Model Bot)

Blender assets for Roblox; **fewer than 20,000 triangles per mesh**. Split large cliffs, buildings and assemblies into reusable meshes. Units are studs (1 stud = 0.3 m), +Y up, +X east, -Z north. Dimensions are [X, Y, Z] unless explicitly labelled as X/Z footprints. All mesh visuals have collision disabled. **Collider dressing** fits the invisible `holes.json` part exactly at felt level; visual-only pieces add no gameplay collision. Moving assemblies need separate meshes with origins at their specified animation pivots.

Priority: **P1** = playable/readable course; **P2** = strong theming; **P3** = polish.

`holes.json` controls hole dimensions, positions, flags, hazards, teleports, colliders and motion. `world/world.json` supplies world-only pieces where no holes.json record exists. Hole coordinates are local; world coordinates are explicitly labelled. Convert using world = R_y(world_yaw) × local + world_offset. Decorative sizes labelled approximate do not override collision. Missing dimensions or motion values remain unspecified rather than invented.

H2, H4, H9, H14 and H17 are excluded while being redesigned. World infrastructure remains in scope, including paths and rides connecting those zones.

Palette: green felt `#4FA83D`, raised felt `#86C24A`, slot felt `#3B7F35`; sandstone rails `#E39A82`, timber `#8A5A33`, rope `#C9A46A`; sand `#D88E5C` / mesa sand `#E59C7C`; peppermint `#D9343A` / `#FFF8F0`; crystals `#FF6FA8` / `#FFB347`; cactus `#3E8E41`, swirl `#F78FB3`, fruit `#FFD84A` / `#9BDB4D` / `#E8304A`; water `#3FB7C9`, foam `#E6FAFF`, teleport glow `#D8FF6A`. **Flags use JSON red `#e03c31`.** Strata cycle every 6 world-Y studs: `#E8736B`, `#F6E3C8`, `#D9534F`, `#F2B48A`, `#C8463F`, `#FBEAD6` (index floor(worldY/6) mod 6). Use sandstone/sand, subtle matte felt, low-gloss candy and glass crystals (Transparency 0.25, Reflectance 0.1, faint Neon core). Sunset gold `#FFD36B`, rose sky `#F7A1A6`, tangerine horizon `#F28C28`, lilac haze `#B49AC9`.
Checked against the current source files and the latest playtest entry, **Round 6 (p6)**. Source paths below use zero-based array indices; IDs/names identify reusable instances. **Visual only** includes trigger/hazard markers: those dress a sensor, not a solid collider. `decoration_anchors[].occluders` are sightline envelopes, not collision. Static mesh origins use the listed base or box centre; felt origins are TOP-surface centres. A sloped felt piece uses its own yawed axes and end heights center.y ± rise/2. Preserve all openings and ball routes. Overhead dressing clears felt/walkways by at least 8 studs; flags and intentional low gameplay cues are separate. Preserve JSON occluder envelopes and tee-to-flag visibility. Rail heights below are collision-box heights, which can extend below felt; do not replace them with generic 1-stud boxes.

## 1) World pieces

All coordinates in this section are **world-space**. World meshes are **visual only; holes.json mapping: none** unless explicitly overridden below. Terrain and player-support collision are a builder responsibility from `world/world.json`; they are not new golf colliders. Cut terrain/dressing away from the authoritative hole surfaces, low greens, open bridge gaps and H16 underpass. World rides remain included even when they connect a redesign hole.
