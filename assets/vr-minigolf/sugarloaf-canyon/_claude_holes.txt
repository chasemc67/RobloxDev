All 18 Sugarloaf Canyon holes are specced in `holes.json` (same schema as Lantern Grove), and the final round passes every Lantern Grove target on every hole: zero traps, zero soft-locks, zero escapes, zero timeouts, and every flag visible from its tee. One thing changed from the plan: **total par is 53, not 54.** H15 Sugar Dune plays as a par 3; why below.

It took 6 sim rounds, all logged in `playtest-log.md` in LG's format. Nothing in `../lantern-grove/` was touched and nothing was posted or sent.

On every hole: an ace line exists, and stroke-cap hits stay at or under 0.7%. The timing obstacles stop the intended shot on 33–46% of phases, all head-on stops (no glancing kicks). The pipe, geode and lift exits never fly past the cup.

**Changes from the plan, and two sim changes:**
- **H15 par 4 → 3.** Every par-4 version I tried either played under par − 0.7 for good players, lost its ace line, or punished average players too hard. I also tried making H13 the par 4 instead, and that failed too. `course.md` (which calls its pars design targets) and `world.json` are updated. Getting 54 back would need a longer H15 footprint, not more tuning.
- **H13 was rebuilt as a walled maze** and **H15 as one tilted dune plane**; several other holes were reworked along the way.
- **Sim additions:** a rectangular trigger so the whole H11 lock chamber acts as the lift, and one new rest rule (a ball that hasn't moved 0.01 studs in 0.5 s is at rest). The rule fixed balls frozen against bank edges in the H5 half-pipe. Physics tests, now 15, all pass.
- **World:** `build_world.py` now reads the real cups and pars from `holes.json`, and `validate.py` checks every felt and rail corner against its footprint. Footprints and placements are unchanged, and validation is CLEAN (iteration 7).

**Caveats:**
- **Needs Studio calibration.** The five moving obstacles and the H11 lift are only modelled in the sim. The stop-vs-kick correction comes from one Lantern Grove pendulum measurement. The exact test shots for the builder are in `timing-p6.json`.
- **H11 lift:** the sim moves the ball to the top instantly at half its entry speed (1.5–7 studs/s). The game platform has to reproduce that exactly; the ace needs the full 7.0 exit speed.
- **Holes close to the limits** may shift in a real playtest: H7 (a single 0.3°-wide ace line), H8 (gap +0.36), H13 (good 2.35 against a 2.3 floor), H5 (only 6 ace grid hits).
- **H5's floor plates run under the slot rock**, so the wall meshes need to hide everything outside the rails.

I also ported `sweep.py` (which merges LG's three sweep scripts and adds the stop/kick split), `summarize.py` (now with a target check) and `debug_plays.py` into `tools/`; all are documented in `tools/README.md`.

Separately, several claude.ai connectors (Canva, Linear, Notion, PostHog, Stripe, Supabase, Vercel, LegalZoom) need authorizing in your claude.ai connector settings before they can be used; none were needed here.
