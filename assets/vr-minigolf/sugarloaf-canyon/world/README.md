# Sugarloaf Canyon: world layout (concept stage)

One continuous canyon world for the 18 Sugarloaf Canyon holes, built as stacked mesa tiers joined by walkways, stairs, rope bridges, lifts
and a gondola. Everything here is generated from `world.json`, and `world.json` is generated by `tools/build_world.py`. The tools are adapted
from `../../lantern-grove/world/tools/`. All 18 holes are now specced in `../holes.json` (status `specced`); `build_world.py` reads each hole's real cup position and par from it, and `validate.py` checks that every holes.json felt and rail corner lies inside the hole's concept pads.

**World:** terrain 1280 x 1280 studs (384 x 384 m), x -640..640, z -640..640, heights 11.1..195.6. Backdrop pieces go out to r ~1500 and the
sky to 2400. **Walk from spawn to the H18 cup: 2700 studs (810 m)**, plus 4 lifts (+164 total) and the Sunset Gondola home (−172).
Validation after the Addendum 5 dome change: **2 visibility failures**, with 3 expected slot-canyon warnings. All geometry, paths, corrected cup heights and teleport checks pass. See `validation.md`.

## Conventions

- **Units/axes**: studs, **1 stud = 0.3 m**, +Y up, +X east, **-Z north** (map up). Bearings are compass degrees (0 = north = -Z, 90 = east = +X).
  These are the same axes as Lantern Grove's holes.json and world.json.
- **Hole placement** (`holes[].placement = {origin:[x,y,z], yaw_deg}`): apply to every local point of the hole
  ```
  x' =  lx*cos(yaw) + lz*sin(yaw) + origin.x
  z' = -lx*sin(yaw) + lz*cos(yaw) + origin.z
  y' =  ly + origin.y            piece_yaw_world = piece.yaw + yaw
  ```
  This is a rotation about +Y (positive = counter-clockwise on the map), then a translation. In Roblox:
  `holeRoot = CFrame.new(origin) * CFrame.Angles(0, math.rad(yaw_deg), 0)`, `part.CFrame = holeRoot * localCFrame`, and axes rotate only.
  The local origin is **the tee**, and play runs toward **local -Z**: yaw 0 plays north, 180 south, -90 east and 90 west (no hole plays west, because the sun is there).
- **Footprints** (`holes[].concept`; `holes[].status` = `specced` once holes.json has the hole, else `concept_footprint`):
  - `pads`: flat boxes in the hole's local frame: `{id, x:[x0,x1], z:[z0,z1], y}` (y = offset from origin.y). A single pad spans local
    z +6 (tee standing room) to -L. Holes with a level change have two tiers (H10 and H16 drop 3.6; H11 rises 4).
  - `spans`: local z ranges where the hole bridges a gap or river, so the ground stays natural (H3 over the Sugarloaf River, H7 over Taffy Gap).
  - `cup_local`: an approximate cup, used as the start of the walkway to the next tee.
  - **Rule for the hole designer:** every felt piece, rail and near-field prop of holes.json must fit inside the hole's pads
    (spans excepted), with the tee at the local origin.
- **Terrain** is a deterministic heightfield `h(x,z)` (`tools/worldlib.py height_at`), evaluated in the order written in `terrain.method`:
  1. base;
  2. regions in list order: `set` (mesa tiers: polygon/ellipse with a cliff falloff), `hill` (Old Sugarloaf, knolls), `slot` (slot canyons carved along a polyline);
  3. rim;
  4. hole pads;
  5. walkway grading;
  6. pad clamp;
  7. water carve.

  Stream and lake banks only carve out to `bank_reach`. An unbounded bank cone once shaved Old Sugarloaf down to y 86; see validation iteration 1.
- **Elevation tiers**: canyon floor 18 · slot floors 18/22/26 · Taffy Mesa + West Ledge 70 · buttes 96/104 · Reservoir Bench 116 (water 108) ·
  Hoodoo Bench 150 · Sugarloaf Crown 190 · Old Sugarloaf summit 128.
- **Strata** (visual): cliffs are banded every 6 studs of world y in the 6 `terrain.strata.colors`, cycling. Use the band index `floor(y/6) mod 6`
  to pick the striped rock-face kit piece. Then every cliff's stripes line up across the world.

  bed depth 3, banks rise 0.8 per stud out to 10 studs. Fill Water from the bed to `level`, which is linear between vertices.
- **Licorice Creek** runs west in Licorice Slot (level 46.5 → 45), drops off Taffy Mesa as **Licorice Falls** (45 → 16.5) and crosses the
  floor to join the river (16.0).
- **Fizzwater Creek** crosses the Hoodoo Bench (148.6 → 148), drops as **Fizzwater Falls** (148 → 113.6), then a short cascade into the reservoir.
- **Sugarloaf Reservoir**: ellipse centre (0, -425), radii 190 x 112, water y 108, 4-stud beach. It's held at its south tip by **Peppermint Dam**:
  x -44..44, centre line z -305, 10 thick, crest y 118 with the walkway at 116. The dam spans the notch in the bench edge. **Spillway Falls**:
  a 20-wide sheet from 108 to 16.8 (drop 91) into the river's plunge pool.

## Walkways, lifts, teleports

Each walkway runs from the cup to the next tee. It leaves the green over the rail (`on_hole`) and reaches the tee from behind (a walk-in point
3 studs outside the footprint). Segment types: `walk`/`ramp` (≤ 10%), `stairs` (rise 0.8, tread 1.5, ≤ 10 steps per flight, 4-stud landings),
`landing`, `bridge` (railed rope or plank bridge, ≤ 5% slope), `dam_crest`, `lift` (vertical cage, boarding points ≤ 30 apart, rise ≤ 60)
and `gondola`. Width is 6, or 8 for the Junction Walk and Dam Crest Walk. The crest has 8 clear studs between solid parapets. **`rails`** on a segment lists the sides that need a railing because the ground drops more than 4 studs within
2.5 studs of the edge. Bridges and the dam crest always have both. The terrain is graded under walk, ramp and stair segments only.

Taffy Lift Top, Dam East Overlook, Hoodoo Lift Top and Crown Lift Top. Each is on flat ground, ≤ 14 studs from a walkway and clear of every hole.

## Sun

`sun`: in-game golden hour, bearing 277°, elevation 25°, ClockTime ~16.3 as a Studio calibration starting point. **Rule:** no tee plays within 45° of the sun bearing. Every hole
plays N, S or E (validated). Golden light is the view from greens, the dam crest and Sunset Point.

`hero_render_sun` is separate and render-only: bearing 277°, elevation +2°. **Flag: the requested +2° is 1° above the arch opening top (+1°); it is not inside the opening (−12°..+1°).** Do not apply hero lighting to the game.

## Validation (tools/validate.py)

The validator checks:
- **Footprints**: they don't overlap (≥ 4 studs between pads of different holes). The closest pair is H5-H6 at 7.0.
- **Footprints on flat ground**: each pad is flat after grading (≤ 0.05) and natural cut/fill is ≤ 6. The worst is H7 at 5.8, at the Taffy Gap rims. Pads aren't
  under water, river carving only happens inside declared spans, and each span really spans something (H3: 6 studs over the river bed; H7: 30 over Taffy Gap).
- **Walkway structure**: walkways are continuous, start at the cup and end at the tee, with the walk-in point ≤ 6 from the footprint.
- **Walkway limits**: grades, stairs, flights and landings; lift rise and offset; lift, gondola, bridge and dam-crest ends on terrain; walkway surface within 0.75 of the terrain.
- **Walkway clearance**: no water without a bridge, ≥ width/2 + 1 from other holes, never crossing its own hole mid-walk, and **railings wherever the ground drops > 4 studs**.
- **View**: the sun isn't in the eyes at any tee, and **Old Sugarloaf is visible from every tee**. The 3 slot-canyon tees are a warning, because they're enclosed by design.
- **Rest of the world**: total walk < 3000; teleport points are valid; scenery stays clear of the footprints; streams flow downhill; the reservoir rim is ≥ water level apart from the dam and the creek mouth; spawn is on the terrain.

## Addendum 5: Old Sugarloaf terrain

The terrain region `old_sugarloaf` now has type `superellipse_dome`, centre [80,18,−130], radius 72, and parameters `{base:18, height:110, radius:72, exponent:4}`. Its formula is `y = 18 + 110*sqrt(max(0, 1-(r/72)^4))`; r is horizontal distance from [80,−130]. Detail noise is suppressed on the dome so the summit is exactly y128. The landmark and its [144,110,144] visual envelope are based at y18.

This requested lower dome is hidden from H17 and H18 tees behind the Crown terrain; the validator retains these two visibility failures rather than silently relaxing the original sightline rule. The H4–H6 enclosed-tee warnings remain.
