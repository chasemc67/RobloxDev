# Pass 5b: render + docs only (Claude, 2026-10-12)

All four steps are done. No FBX, gondola geometry, cliffs, terrain, props, manifest pieces or layout were changed. No collision meshes were made and Codex was not used. Blender 5.2 was only launched through `run_blender.ps1`, one process at a time, 4 runs in total.

## 1. Staged hero `V1-hub-tee1`
- Script: `_src/render_hero_pass5.py`, a copy of `render_hero.py`. The original is untouched and backed up at `backup_pass5b/render_hero.py`. The script never saves over the pass4 `.blend`.
- **Camera:** eye (60, 25.5, 482) in game coords, which is 12 studs behind and 7.5 above the H1 tee at (60, 18, 470). Target (80, -49.6, -130) is the bearing to Old Sugarloaf pitched down 7°. Horizontal FOV 78°, 16:9.
- **Staging**, render-scene-only, built from holes.json with `world = R_y(world_yaw)*local + world_offset`:
  - 126 felt slabs for all 18 holes. Top surface +0.03, planar slopes per the schema, green noise ramp #23962F to #52C24A, fine bump, sheen.
  - 308 rails as timber bodies with peppermint-striped caps. Ramp rails follow the felt height +1.0 at each end.
  - 18 cups (dark liner + white rim) and flags (pole 7.5, banner size and colour from holes.json).
  - A white ball on the H1 tee, drawn at 3× scale (r 0.24) so it is legible.
  - 65 `bumper` volumes were skipped because kit props dress boulders and hoodoos.
- **Light:** key sun at bearing 277 / elevation 7, energy 6.5, colour (1, .55, .24). Orange rim sun at 330/10, energy 2.2. Camera-ray sky #FFB98A peach > #F48A8C coral > #B864A0 mauve > #6A4FA3 violet. Pass4 lavender ambient and fill are kept. AgX +1.35.
- **Render:** Cycles OptiX (RTX 4090), 1920x1080, 128 spp, denoised, 11.7 s.
- **Previews:**
  - Preview 1 (640x360, 16 spp, eye (60, 23.5, 475), FOV 75) was too tight on the H1 lane and the sky was not violet enough, so I pulled back, raised the eye and tightened the sky ramp.
  - Preview 2 framing was OK.
  - In the final the felt read teal, so I saturated the felt ramp and re-rendered V1 only.
- **Outputs:** `previews/hero/V1-hub-tee1_staged.png` and `previews/hero/V1-hub-tee1_vs_concept.png`. The comparison puts the render on the left and the concept `world/vistas/V1-hub-tee1.png` on the right, each labelled.
- **Gaps vs the concept:**
  - The sun at bearing 277 is about 85° left of this north-facing view, so there is no sun disc in frame. The concept places the sun ahead.
  - The kit has no trading-post boardwalk, awnings, barrels or lanterns at the tee.
  - Junction has no river or waterfall in this direction.
  - There are no mesa-top greens or rope bridges on the skyline in this direction.
  - The canyon floor remains broad bare sand around the staged lanes.
