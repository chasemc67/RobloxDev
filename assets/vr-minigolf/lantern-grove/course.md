# Lantern Grove: Theme Bible

VR mini golf course for Roblox. 9 holes, total par 27. Autumn fairy forest at golden hour, with a night variant later.
Reference art: `_ref/C08-lantern-grove.png` (day vista), `_ref/C08n-lantern-grove-night.png` (night vista),
`_ref/L05-overview-lantern-grove.png` (illustrated 9-hole map). Geometry source of truth: `holes.json`
(generated by `tools/build_holes.py`). Layouts: `hNN-layout.png`, `course-map.png`.

Units: studs. **1 stud = 0.3 m**, Y up. Ball radius 0.08 stud, gameplay cup radius 0.5 stud.
Felt lanes are 7–8 studs wide (2.1–2.4 m) and greens 12–18 studs (3.6–5.4 m). Rails are 1 stud high (0.3 m) and 0.5 thick (0.15 m).

## Mood

Cozy, warm and a little magical. It should feel like a woodland fair the fairies set up just for you.
Big, chunky, hand-made shapes: thick roots, round mushroom caps, stacked river stones, paper lanterns everywhere.
Nothing is sharp or scary. Even the hazards are gentle (the water hazard is a clear babbling stream, the pit is a mossy rabbit hole).
In VR the player stands at true scale, so key landmarks (mushroom houses, mill, hollow tree) are big enough to
tower over you (8–60 studs = 2.4–18 m). Rails stay low and soft-cornered so the felt reads clearly.

Story arc, as you walk the course:
1. **Sunny Forest Edge** (H1–H2): open sky, dappled light, maples and a welcome gate. Teaches putting, banks and drops.
2. **Mushroom Village** (H3–H4): red-cap mushroom houses, little bridges and windows. A tunnel through a house and a spinning carousel.
3. **Stream Crossing / Waterfall Terraces** (H5–H7): the stream cuts through rocky banks. Root bridge, water mill, stepped waterfall.
4. **Hollow Tree** (H8–H9): the roots of a giant hollow golden tree. Fireflies, glowing rings, then the lantern-lit heart inside the trunk.

## Materials (Roblox)

- **Felt**: SmoothPlastic or Fabric material, colour per palette with a subtle noise texture. No reflectance.
- **Rails**: visual meshes are stone blocks with oak caps. The collision boxes are invisible parts from `holes.json`.
  Some collision rails next to banks are taller (up to 2.4 studs above the felt) but must stay invisible. The visual rail stays 1 stud high.
- **Wood**: WoodPlanks for bridges and decks. Roots and the tree use a bark texture on Wood.
- **Stone**: Slate or Rock for boulders, cobble for the stream banks.
- **Water**: Glass with transparency 0.4 plus an animated scrolling texture. Waterfalls use Beams/ParticleEmitters.
- **Lanterns**: Neon core (Lantern amber) inside SmoothPlastic paper shells with a PointLight (range 8, brightness 1.2).
  Use SurfaceLight on the big hollow-tree lanterns.
- **Mushroom caps**: SmoothPlastic, Toadstool red with cream decal spots.
- **Teleport rings**: Neon torus (Firefly lime) with a particle swirl.

## Lighting

**Day (launch):**
- Lighting.ClockTime = 16.8 (late golden afternoon). GeographicLatitude 40.
- Ambient `#6B5A48`, OutdoorAmbient `#8C7A60`.
- Brightness 2.2, ColorShift_Top `#FFD9A0`, EnvironmentDiffuse/SpecularScale 0.6.
- Atmosphere: Density 0.32, Color `#F3C98B`, Decay `#B5683A`, Glare 0.3, Haze 1.6. This gives warm, hazy depth like C08.
- Sky: soft cumulus with a warm sun. Bloom intensity 0.6, threshold 1.4. SunRays intensity 0.08.
- Lanterns are on but subtle (brightness 0.6).
- Leaf ParticleEmitters (drifting maple leaves) near each hole's landmark.

**Night (later variant, see C08n):**
- ClockTime 21.5. Full moon placed behind the hollow tree.
- Ambient `#1E2350`, OutdoorAmbient `#3A4A8C`, Brightness 0.6.
- Atmosphere Density 0.4, Color `#2B3470`, Haze 2.
- Lanterns at full brightness (1.6, range 10). Small Neon lights inset into the rail caps every ~4 studs, like C08n.
- Firefly particles (Firefly lime, LightEmission 1) near the stream and in Hollow Tree zones.
- Teleport rings and the H9 heart get extra glow. Water gets moonlight specular.

## Hero props (see props.md for the full list)

1. **Giant hollow golden tree**: course finale and skyline anchor, visible from every hole.
2. **Mushroom houses** (red caps, round windows, little doors). One has a felt tunnel through its stem (H3).
3. **Lantern torii gate** at H1. It is the course's welcome arch.
4. **Water mill with turning paddle wheel** (H6 moving part).
5. **Arched root bridge and covered plank bridge** (H5).
6. **Puffball carousel spinner** (H4 moving part).
7. **Three-step waterfall cascade** (H7).
8. **Firefly teleport rings** in a root knot (H8) and the secret Lantern Knot (H9).
9. **Hanging paper lanterns**: everywhere, in 3 sizes.

## Flow of the 9 holes

| # | Hole | Zone | Par | Landmark | Gimmick |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lantern Gate | Sunny Forest Edge | 2 | Lantern torii gate | Gentle ramp, then a 45° deflector bank onto an offset green |
| 2 | Acorn Ledge | Sunny Forest Edge | 2 | Giant acorn on a stump | Drop between levels through one of two chutes; rabbit-hole pit |
| 3 | Toadstool Row | Mushroom Village | 3 | Mushroom house with a stem tunnel | S-bend with two steep banked-plane corners plus a tunnel |
| 4 | Spore Spinner | Mushroom Village | 3 | Puffball carousel | Rotating 2-arm spinner bar, then a ramp up to a raised green |
| 5 | Rootbridge Crossing | Stream Crossing | 3 | Arched root bridge | Split path: rail-less root bridge (risk) or covered bridge (safe) over water |
| 6 | Mill Wheel Run | Stream Crossing | 3 | Water mill | Horizontal-axle paddle wheel across the flume, deflector boards, mill-race hazard |
| 7 | Cascade Steps | Waterfall Terraces | 3 | Three-step waterfall | Two drops down terraces, sliding log, waterfall-pool hazard, side-sloped green |
| 8 | Firefly Hollow | Hollow Tree Roots | 4 | Firefly ring in a root knot | Teleport-ring shortcut or the long route (banked corners, long ramp) |
| 9 | Heart of the Grove | Inside the Hollow Tree | 4 | Lantern-lit heart of the tree | Ramp and bank, rising root gate, tunnel, deflector, lantern bar, drop into the heart, secret knot teleport |

Difficulty ramps from intro banks and drops (H1–H2) through timing obstacles (H4, H6, H7) to multi-gimmick par 4s (H8–H9).
The walking path (dashed on `course-map.png`) runs from each cup to the next tee. The stream runs north–south
through the middle of the map, past H5's crossing, H6's mill race and H7's pool. The hollow tree sits east, and H8 and H9 wrap around and into it.

## Build notes for RobloxDevBot

- Build collision straight from `holes.json` (all parts invisible, Anchored, CanCollide). Felt pieces are boxes whose
  **top-surface centre** is `center`. Slopes are given as `rise` along the local axis (see the `schema` block).
- Set `Workspace.Gravity = 32.7`. Ball physics constants are in the `physics` block. Max putt speed is 19.78 studs/s (5.9 m/s),
  which rolls 60 studs (18 m) on flat felt.
- Moving parts are separate anchored parts animated by CFrame (TweenService or Heartbeat) about `pivot`/`axis` at `speed`.
  The H6 wheel paddles dip below the felt, so cut a 0.8-stud slot in the channel felt under the wheel (the visual felt only;
  the collision felt can stay whole, because a paddle that low never blocks the ball).
- Teleports: when the ball touches the entry ring trigger, CFrame it to the exit and set its velocity to
  `dir * clamp(speed*speed_factor, min_speed, max_speed)`.
