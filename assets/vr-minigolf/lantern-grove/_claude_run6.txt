Widening along the swing makes things worse. A longer box has to hang higher to clear the felt mid-swing, so it meets the ball (centre 0.08 above the felt) above its middle and lets it through. Turning the foot to yaw 0 lets it sit lower, which is what raises the block rate.

**Mesh fit: no.** Seen from above, the foot's corners stick out up to 0.12 past two faces of the 1.6×1.6 lantern body (turned 30°). For 3D Model Bot:
- **Preferred:** add a base plinth to the lantern mesh, 0.9 × 0.4 × 1.6, turned -30° relative to the body, centred under it, with its bottom 0.062 below the body's bottom.
- **Alternative:** widen the lantern's bottom 0.4 to a 1.84 × 1.84 square at the same 30° and extend it 0.062 lower. This also covers the foot, but leaves some visible base with no collider behind it.

Without one of these, the invisible foot would stop balls just outside the visible lantern.
