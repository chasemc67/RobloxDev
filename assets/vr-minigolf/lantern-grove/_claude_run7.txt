The fix is to change the H9 pendulum's swing from ±13° to **±11°**. Nothing else on the pendulum moves, and no new mesh work is needed. I expect Studio to block about **44% (roughly 21/48)**, against 31% now.

**Why widening didn't help.** The sim's "blocks" are really two different things:
- **Stops:** the ball hits the foot's north face, the one square to the shot line, and bounces straight back.
- **Kicks:** a side face of the foot, or a dipping corner of the lantern body, throws the ball sideways. Studio carries many of these on through.

Studio fits "all stops plus about 36% of kicks" across both measurements: 22.4 predicted vs 23/72 measured. So the only reliable lever is how long that square face sits over the shot line, and a smaller swing does that (22% of the cycle, up from 18%).

I first tried modelling the carry as friction, but it made the sim block *more*, so I took it back out of `sim.py`.

**Chosen change (H9 only)**
- **Swing:** angle(t) = **11°**·sin(360°·t/4 s). Period 4 s, phase 0 and hinge (21.2, 8.996, -38.5) are unchanged.
- **Lantern body collider:** 1.6×1.8×1.6, yaw 30, centre (0, -7.0, 0) below the hinge. Unchanged.
- **Foot collider:** 1.0×0.4×1.6, yaw 0, centre (0, -7.759, 0), which is what Studio v131 already has. Its bottom is 0.037 above the felt at rest and at least 0.021 over the whole swing (all 8 corners of both colliders checked).
- **Sweep:** shrinks to x 18.62–23.78 (was 18.36–24.04), so every beam, rail and lantern clearance holds or grows.
- At the ends of the swing the lantern still clears the ball on the shot line by at least 0.15.
