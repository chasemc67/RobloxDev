# Aurora Archipelago: world layout (concept stage)

One continuous world for the 18 Aurora Archipelago holes: a ring of floating snow-capped islands at six heights, climbing
anticlockwise round the Starfrost Spire over a cloud sea. Walkways, the Lantern Stair, three railed bridges, four slow lifts and a
gondola join the islands. Everything here is generated from `world.json`, and `world.json` is generated by `tools/build_world.py`.
The tools are adapted from `../../sugarloaf-canyon/world/tools/`. **Phase 2:** `../holes.json` now specs all 18 holes inside these footprints; `build_world.py` reads the real cups from it and `validate.py` checks the fit (iteration 13, CLEAN).

**World:** terrain 1320 x 1320 studs (396 x 396 m), x -660..660, z -660..660, heights 0 (cloud floor, void) and 60..242 on the islands.
Far islands go out to r ~960, distant island-mountains to r ~1600, and the sky to 2600. **Walk from spawn to the H18 cup: 1904 studs
(571 m)**, plus 4 lifts (+138 total) and the Snowpuff Sky Gondola home (−162). The validator is **CLEAN** with 0 warnings (`validation.md`).

## Conventions

- **Units/axes**: studs, **1 stud = 0.3 m**, +Y up, +X east, **-Z north** (map up). Bearings are compass degrees (0 = north = -Z, 90 = east = +X).
  These are the same axes as Lantern Grove and Sugarloaf Canyon.
- **Hole placement** (`holes[].placement = {origin:[x,y,z], yaw_deg}`): apply to every local point of the hole
  ```
  x' =  lx*cos(yaw) + lz*sin(yaw) + origin.x
  z' = -lx*sin(yaw) + lz*cos(yaw) + origin.z
  y' =  ly + origin.y            piece_yaw_world = piece.yaw + yaw
  ```
  This is a rotation about +Y (positive = counter-clockwise on the map), then a translation. In Roblox:
  `holeRoot = CFrame.new(origin) * CFrame.Angles(0, math.rad(yaw_deg), 0)`, `part.CFrame = holeRoot * localCFrame`, and axes rotate only.
  The local origin is **the tee**, and play runs toward **local -Z**: yaw 0 plays north, 90 west, 180 south, -90 east.
  When holes.json exists, world.json placement supersedes any `world_offset` in it.
- **Transform check**: yaw 90 turns local -Z to world -X. H10 (origin [510, 146, 60], yaw 90): local [0, 0, -50] →
  world **[460, 146, 60]**, which is 50 studs west of the tee. H3 (origin [-40, 60, 470], yaw -45): local cup [0, 0, -62] →
  world [3.84, 60, 426.16], to the north-east.
- **Footprints** (`holes[].concept`, `status: concept_footprint`):
  - `pads`: flat boxes in the hole's local frame, `{id, x:[x0,x1], z:[z0,z1], y}` (y = offset from origin.y), from local z +6
    (tee standing room) to -L. H5 has two tiers (rises +4), H15 has two (drops −4).
  - `spans`: local z ranges where the hole bridges **open sky**, so there's no ground below (H3 Glimmer Gap, H17 Aurora Gap, both 26 long).
    Each span carries a `player_guard` (player-only barrier on both gap rims and along rail-less ball bridges) and a `player_deck`
    (a railed 4-wide walk-on deck beside the safe ball bridge).
  - `cup_local`: the approximate cup, where the walkway to the next tee starts. `tee_teleport`: the next-tee teleport arrival pad, 3 studs behind the tee box.
  - **Rule for the hole designer:** every felt piece, rail and near-field prop of holes.json must fit inside the hole's pads (spans
    excepted), with the tee at the local origin.
- **Terrain** is a deterministic heightfield `h(x,z)` (`tools/worldlib.py height_at`), evaluated in the order written in `terrain.method`:
  1. base: the cloud floor, y 0;
  2. regions in list order. Islands are `set` regions sorted low → high. Their shapes are boxes round every footprint (14-stud margin,
     split at spans), capsules round every walk leg (r 9), discs round hubs, and zone bodies. Edges wobble on the signed distance, with a 6-stud cliff falloff;
     16 decorative islets, then 26 snowdrift/crag `hill`s;
  3. detail noise on island tops only;
  4. hole pads (flatten, blend back; never raise ground along a span strip);
  5. walkway grading (graded segments only), then (5a) teleport flats, then (5b) the pad clamp;
  6. lagoon and runoff carve (banks bounded by `bank_reach`).
- **Void**: any `h < void.terrain_fill_min_y` (30) means **no terrain at all**. The cloud floor isn't walkable. The cloud deck mesh
  is at y 36, and the respawn plane at y 40.
- **Elevation tiers**: Lanternfall Landing 60 · Steamwhistle Lagoons 84 (water 82.8) · Spire Isle 100 · Candyfrost Reef 112 ·
  Driftfloe Shelf 146 · Icefall Terraces + Aurora Landing 182 · Skylight Summit 222 · islets 48-250 · Spire tip 340.

## Walkways, bridges, lifts, teleports

Each walkway runs from the cup to the next tee. It leaves the green over the rail (`on_hole`) and reaches the tee **from behind** (the walk-in
point is 3 studs behind the tee box, validated as local z ≥ 6). Segment types are `walk`/`ramp` (≤ 10%), `stairs` (rise 0.8,
tread 1.5, ≤ 10 steps per flight, 4-stud landings), `landing`, `bridge` (railed, `style` ice or aurora, ≤ 5%), `lift` and `gondola`.
Width is 6, or 8 for the Quay Walk. **`rails`** lists the sides that need a 3.5-stud railing because the ground drops more than
4 studs within **5.5 studs** of the edge (`path_rules.rail_reach`, stricter than the recipe's 2.5). Bridges always have both.
Brass lantern posts stand every ~16 studs, 0.75 outside the walkway edge (`lantern_posts`, 89 of them), and never over the walkway.

Lifts are open cages at a constant **3 studs/s**, eased, jolt-free and railed all round. The player stands still, with railed gangways
to both boarding points. The gondola is a seated, enclosed cabin; its cable mast is 16 above each station, and the cabin keeps ≥ 8 clear
of every surface (validated). Every ride has a "teleport instead" pad. **Teleport pads** (`teleport_points`, 4 x 4, flattened into the
heightfield as `terrain.flats`): Lantern Quay Plaza, Lantern Stair Top, Steam Lift Top, Coral Lift Top, Aurora Landing,
Skybridge West, Crystal Lift Top and Skylight Point. There's one hub pad per zone; each is flat, ≤ 14 from a walkway and clear of
every hole. Pads and viewpoints within reach of a drop carry `parapet: true`; the Aurora Landing pinnacle gets a full perimeter parapet.

## Moon and aurora

`moon`: bearing 220° (SW), elevation 14°, the low moon plus a twilight band. **Rule:** no tee plays within 45° of it. Every hole plays
N, NE, E or W (validated; the W tees are 50° off). `aurora`: animated ribbons at bearings 300-60°, elevations 25-75°, emissive only.
The night lighting values are in `../course.md`.

## Validation (tools/validate.py)

The validator checks:
- **Footprints**: no overlap (≥ 4 studs; closest pair H2-H3 at 9.8), ≥ 60 from the terrain edge, pads flat (≤ 0.05) and natural
  cut/fill ≤ 6 (worst 4.2, at the H5/H15 tier steps). Pads aren't under water and are ≥ 10 above the respawn plane. Each span really spans open sky
  (both 60+ studs deep) and carries a player guard and a railed deck.
- **Walkways**: continuity, start at the cup, end at the tee, walk-in point behind the tee; grades, stairs, flights and landings; lift
  rise ≤ 60 and offset ≤ 30; lift, bridge and gondola ends on terrain; surface within 0.75 of the terrain; **never over the void**
  unless bridged; no water; ≥ width/2 + 1 from other holes; never crossing their own hole.
- **Railings and parapets**: rails wherever the ground drops > 4 within 5.5 studs of a walkway edge, bridges railed on both sides at
  ≥ 3.5, pad edges next to a drop (none: every pad has ≥ 11 studs of apron), teleport pads and viewpoints near a drop.
- **Head clearance ≥ 8 studs** over every felt sample (2-stud grid) and walkway sample (1-stud): backdrop pieces (arches use their
  `clear_height`), other walkways and bridges passing overhead, the gondola cabin (cable − 6) and lantern posts.
- **Comfort**: moon angle per tee; lifts ≤ 4 studs/s, gondola ≤ 6, all railed and jolt-free with a teleport alternative; a hub
  teleport pad in every zone; a flat next-tee teleport pad behind every tee; forced locomotion off; void not walkable.
- **View**: Starfrost Spire line of sight from every tee eye (tee + 5.5) to its tip, upper and waist. **The tip is visible from all 18 tees.**
- **Variety**: 18 distinct headline gimmicks; the 6 movers have 6 distinct motion types.
- **World**: total walk < 3000; consecutive holes connected, spawn → H1, H18 → hub; scenery clear of footprints and on island ground;
  the runoff flows downhill; lagoon rims hold the water (except the outlet channel); spawn on terrain.
