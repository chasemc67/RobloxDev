I've written both files. `props.md` is 11,583 bytes (`wc -c`), under the 15 KB limit, and `props-full.md` is 138,482 bytes. Every number comes straight from holes.json and world.json; neither file was changed, nor were tools or the other courses.

**What I did:** a script, `_work/gen_props.py`, reads the two JSON files and writes `props-full.md`. I wrote `props.md` by hand from its output. Nothing was posted, no images were made and Codex wasn't used.

**props.md:** a short P1/P2/P3 list. It has the 6 movers, a table of all 18 landmarks with placements and cups, the reusable kits, the plinths, and short P2 and P3 lists.

**Triangle budgets:** the near-field estimates run from 17.5k (H4) to 33.1k (H7), all under the 34k cap. To stay under, I set my own per-prop targets: rail 25 tris per stud, palm 600, coral 450, snowpuff 500. If the modeler goes over those, the Reef and Lagoon holes (H5–H8) will exceed 34k first.

**Mismatches you should know about** (listed in §13 of `props-full.md`):
- **Halo Arch:** holes.json puts it at world z -284, 4 deep; world.json says z -292, 8 deep. I told the modeler to use holes.json, because the flag line-of-sight check was run against it.
- **Steamwhistle Geyser:** there are two records 32.7 studs apart: the H5 cone (6×7×6) and the world.json cone (14×10×14). I suggested one mesh used twice. You need to decide which one does the 60-stud steam puff.
- **Views, not props:** the Great Frozen Falls and Glimmer Islet positions in the hole files don't match world.json. I treated the hole entries as viewing references and placed the meshes from world.json.
- **Out-of-date mover numbers:** the "starting spec" mover table in course.md is older than holes.json; I used holes.json throughout.
- **Shape conflicts:**
  - The H5 platform is described as hexagonal but its collider is a 3×3 square. I specified square contact faces with the hex look only on the top and trim.
  - The H11 floe is 0.636 thick in holes.json, not the 0.6 in its description; I used 0.636.
