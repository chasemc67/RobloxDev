# Aurora Archipelago: complete prop list (props-full.md)

Complete inventory for the 3D modeler and the builder. Generated by `_work/gen_props.py` from **`holes.json`** (hole geometry, movers, anchors; source of truth) and **`world/world.json`** (placement, backdrop, paths, lifts). The concise build list is `props.md`. Course: 18 holes, par 53; physics and units as in `course.md`.

- **Units/axes:** studs (1 stud = 0.3 m), +Y up, +X east, -Z north. Sizes are bounding boxes **[x, y, z]** in studs unless labelled X/Z footprint.
- **Hole-local coordinates** unless labelled *world*. Tee = local origin, play toward local -Z. world = R_y(yaw) x local + origin: `x' = lx*cos(yaw) + lz*sin(yaw) + ox`, `z' = -lx*sin(yaw) + lz*cos(yaw) + oz`, `y' = ly + oy`. Roblox: `holeRoot = CFrame.new(origin) * CFrame.Angles(0, math.rad(yaw), 0)`, `part.CFrame = holeRoot * localCFrame`. Placements from `world.json holes[].placement` (identical to `holes.json world_offset/world_yaw`, checked).
- **Every prop is decoration only, CanCollide off.** Ball collision comes only from the invisible holes.json parts (felt, walls, movers, hazards, teleports). Player-only collision (railings, decks, guards, stairs) comes from `vr_comfort` and `world.json`; those are builder parts, not mesh collision.
- **Collider dressing** = a mesh that skins an invisible holes.json box: its contact faces must coincide with the box faces from felt level up to the box top (no bigger bases, no lips into the lane, no gaps the ball can see into). Decoration may rise above the box top only where the notes allow.
- **Felt `center` is the TOP surface centre**; wall/blocker `center` is the BOX centre. Anchor `position` is the prop's base centre.
- **Priorities:** **P1** = playable and readable (gameplay surfaces, movers, flags, cues, signs, landmarks that explain the hole); **P2** = strong theming; **P3** = polish.

Palette (from `course.md`): felt `#4FA83D` / raised `#86C24A` / shadow `#2F7A3A`; ice felt `#9FE3F2`; snow `#F4F6FF` / shadow `#C9C3EC`; slate-violet rock `#5E6177`, basalt `#3A3550`; timber `#8A5A33`, snow cap `#FFFFFF`; brass `#C9A04A`, ember `#FFB861`; glacier cyan `#8FE3FF`, crystal core `#BFF6FF`; aurora `#5CFFB0` / `#3DE2C8` / `#B07CFF` / `#FF7AD9`; lagoon `#2FD6C8`, steam `#E9FBFF`; coral `#FF6FB5` / `#FFA552` / `#6FFFD2`; palm `#2E8B6A` + frost `#E6FBFF`; pine `#2F6B4F`; snowpuff `#222233` / `#FFF4E2` / `#FF9A3C`; flag `#E03C31`; teleport/respawn glow `#D8FF6A`.

## 1. Mesh conventions

- 1 Blender unit = 1 stud. FBX export **forward -Z, up Y**, apply transforms, so props arrive upright at true size in the holes.json frame.
- **Origins:** static props at the **base centre** (= anchor `position`); moving props at the **exact holes.json pivot** (section 4), so the builder drives mesh and invisible blockers with the same CFrame formula. Collider skins: origin at the skinned box centre unless stated.
- One mesh per material; each material has a flat-colour fallback (palette above). Glass-look ice: Transparency 0.3, Reflectance 0.1 with a Neon `#BFF6FF` core mesh; aurora pieces Neon + animated mint->violet gradient; coral SmoothPlastic + Neon core; felt SmoothPlastic/Fabric, no reflectance.
- Chunky, rounded, hand-made shapes (thick soft snow caps, bevelled timber, fat brass fittings). No text baked into meshes except signs (separate SurfaceGui or decal).
- Separate meshes for anything animated (flag banners, snowpuff heads, steam lamps, toggles, mover parts). Snowpuff heads are a separate child mesh pivoted at the neck so the script can turn them to follow the ball.
- Decals (felt arrows, chevrons, ripples, respawn stones) are flush quads 0.01 thick on the felt top, no collision; never raise felt.
- Previews: eye height 5.5 with a 5-stud reference capsule, a 3/4 view, and a VR tee view of each hole over the real world layout.
- Export names: `aa_<hole>_<prop>` for per-hole props (e.g. `aa_h12_paddle_wheel`), `aa_kit_<name>` for reused kit, `aa_world_<id>` for world pieces.

## 3. Clash rules for dressing

- **Nothing from felt +1.25 up to felt +8 over any hole** (recipe band 6.5 raised to the course's **8-stud head clearance**, `world.json comfort.decoration_rule`). Exceptions are only the gameplay movers (H12 wheel, H13 arms, H14 bell: below 4 above felt except the bell hanger/gantry beam at y >= 10) and the felt-level cues. Lanterns over greens, lintels, canopies, arches and aurora ribbons have their **underside >= 8** over the felt (H2 lintels y 8, H2 lantern cue 9, H13 canopy 9, H14 beam 10.35, H18 arch opening 18, H17 ribbon >= 10).
- **Walkways** keep width/2 + 0.75 clear sideways and 8 high; lantern posts stand 0.75 outside the walkway edge; nothing on stairs, landings, lift floors, bridges or teleport pads.
- **Plants** >= 1.5 studs from every rail collider; **tall props (> 4 high)** >= 3 studs from rails unless listed with an exact anchor here (anchors are already fitted).
- **Nothing in water/pools, hazards, pits or gap volumes** (H3 Glimmer Gap and H17 Aurora Gap: x -13..13, z -24..-50 / -16..-42 local, down to the cloud deck), nothing inside the cup radius 0.5, nothing inside teleport entry/exit zones (H9 ring r 0.3; H5 platform 3x3).
- **Sightlines:** keep the tee-eye (tee + 5.5) to flag-top line clear; anchor `occluders` are the solid envelopes the sim validated - stay inside them.
- **Hero shells:** H2 burrows are open-topped (no roofs), H12 icebreaker hull keeps its slot clear (2.4 wide channel), H13 canopy keeps outside dressing out of the r 11.5 sweep.
- **Mover clearances:** nothing static within the swept volume of any mover (section 4) + 0.15 studs (matches `collision_cuts.clearance`).
- **Player-only guards** (`vr_comfort.player_guards`) are invisible PlayerGuard parts; dressing must not suggest a ball rail there (no solid timber rails along the H3 plank or H17 light span).
