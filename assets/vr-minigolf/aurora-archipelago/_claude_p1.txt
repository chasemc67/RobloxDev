## Aurora Archipelago: Phase 1 done, world validator CLEAN

All five deliverables are written, and the validator reports **CLEAN with 0 warnings** after 8 iterations, each logged in `world/validation.md` with a "Changes:" note. I looked at `world-map.png` and the labels don't collide: they're placed automatically and never overlap each other or a footprint, with leader lines when pushed out. Nothing was posted or sent, and no image generator was used.

- **World size:** 1320 x 1320 studs (396 m square). Far islands go out to about 960 studs, distant silhouettes to 1600 and the sky to 2600.
- **Zone heights:** the islands climb anticlockwise round the landmark.

- **Cloud floor:** y 0 and not walkable. No terrain below y 30, the cloud deck is at y 36 and the respawn plane at y 40.
- **Landmark:** the Starfrost Spire, a giant aurora-crystal spire on its own island at (0, −40). It's 240 studs tall with its tip at y 340, and the tip is visible from all 18 tees.
- **Walk total:** 1904 studs (571 m) from spawn to the H18 cup, against a target under 3000. On top of that:
  - four slow lifts, 3 studs/s, +138 in total;
  - the Lantern Stair (+24);
  - three railed bridges: Glimmer Ice, Coral Ice, and the 135-stud Aurora Skybridge;
  - a gondola back down to spawn (−162).
- **Moon:** low in the south-west (bearing 220, elevation 14). No tee plays within 45° of it; the closest is 50° off.
- **VR comfort:**
  - every felt and walkway sample has at least 8 studs of head room;
  - railings go on every walkway side where a drop is within 5.5 studs of the edge (stricter than the recipe's 2.5), and every bridge is railed on both sides;
  - every pad keeps at least 11 studs of snow between it and a cliff edge;
  - the two holes that cross open sky (H3, H17) have player-only guard walls;
  - there's a teleport pad at every zone hub and a next-tee pad behind every tee.

Total par is **53** (27 out, 26 back). The six movers each use a different motion type, and `course.md` says to run timing checks at 96+ phases.

Three things the next phase needs to know:
- **Ice felt on H4 is a new surface.** Its friction has to be measured in Studio and added to the sim before H4 can be tuned. I suggested starting at 0.35× normal deceleration.
- **One validator rule is a judgement call.** The lagoon-rim check now treats the outlet channel's banks as part of the runoff mouth, because that water drains into the runoff. It's noted in `validation.md`.
- **`build_world.py` doesn't read holes.json yet.** It will need the reference course's hook to pick up the real cup positions once that file exists.
